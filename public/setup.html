02Tailscale-
Enable Funnel
One click: installs Tailscale if needed, opens login, and enables Funnel. Public link: <nickname>.ts.net — free, no domain.
  1. Connect installs the bundled Tailscale and opens the Tailscale login. Sign in or create a free account.
  2. If Funnel is off, press Enable Funnel and allow HTTPS certificates + Funnel for this device.
  3. Return here: the status turns green and the public link is live. The free Tailscale plan is enough.
03Twitch—
  1. Open dev.twitch.tv/console/apps → Register Your Application.
  2. Name — any; OAuth Redirect URLs — add: .
  3. Category — any (e.g. Broadcaster Suite); Client Type — Confidential.
  4. Copy the Client ID; click New Secret and copy the Client Secret.
  5. Paste both above and press Save.
  6. Press Authorize Twitch, log in and allow access.
  7. Back here press Save again — the status turns green.
